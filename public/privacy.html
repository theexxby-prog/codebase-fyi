Privacy

Dream Items (list.codebase.fyi) is a private list app for one family. It isn't a public service.

What it keeps

  • Things you add: product links, names, prices, photos and screenshots you choose, places, and notes.
  • If you send a post or reel to the Instagram account @codebase.fyi: the message, the shared post's photo and caption, and your Instagram username. This only happens for accounts @codebase.fyi follows; messages from anyone else are ignored and not kept.

What it does with it

It sorts what you send into your own private list (Shop, See or Eat), looks up prices and details on the web, and replies to your message to say it's saved. Only you can open your list, with your personal link. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Services it uses

Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Anthropic's Claude (reading screenshots and looking things up), and Meta's Instagram API (receiving and replying to messages).

Deleting your data

Remove any item from its page in the app. To delete everything, including your list and anything received from your Instagram account, send "delete my data" to @codebase.fyi on Instagram, and it's removed within 7 days.
